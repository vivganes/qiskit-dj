Quantum DJ Booth 2-qubit edition

Mix 4 songs with 2 qubits — then measure to drop one on the floor

Free play

Build any set you like: up to 10 steps on 2 qubits with H, X, Y, Z and ● (CNOT). Use Share board to send your set to someone else.
